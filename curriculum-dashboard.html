Inspiration

Pirsig, Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance — recognition of quality precedes instruction in technique. Students identify what is good before they are taught how to produce it. The desire to understand technique comes from the desire to replicate quality they have already recognized. Writing is a conduit to thinking, not the end product.
